Playground

Change a parameter. Watch the numbers move.

Three modules built on real implementations rather than screenshots: gradient descent, k-nearest neighbours, a decision stump, byte-level BPE, causal attention and a confusion matrix you can perturb. The dataset is synthetic and says so.

200 synthetic rowsClient-side computationDeterministic seed

Train three models on a synthetic dataset, pick the features, and score a connection you build yourself. Everything runs locally in your browser on a synthetic dataset — no real traffic, no external model calls, nothing to install.
